## Pattern 08 — "Recessional / Coda"

Full circle: Bellows returns with the *exact* same trigger as Pattern 00's opening (`D-4 09 A20`), and Ignition re-enters at the original Nave-era quarter-note density rather than Forge's density — the piece coming home rather than climaxing again. Bars 1–2 reuse the Nave rhythm cell verbatim, and bar 2 layers in one last Choir of Gears statement, in its original Liturgy A ascending shape (G#3→B-3→D-4) with the Mechanic2 response tag — the "hymn" getting one final, complete recitation before the end.

**Bar 3 — convergence.** Kick tightens to 8th notes one more time while Siren descends from the very top of its range (B-4) down through a six-note chromatic run, volume climbing the whole way — falling pitch against rising intensity, a genuine converging-toward-a-point feeling rather than another all-out climax.

**Row 44 — the pause.** A full bar-beat of silence across all four channels: nothing plays. This is the "breath before the flourish" — the deliberate opposite of a fadeout, a hard intake before the final gesture.

**Row 45 — the flourish, and the actual ending.** All four channels fire together on a single unison hit: Mechanic2 (Ch1), linnkick (Ch2), MonsterBass root D-4 (Ch3), Snare7 (Ch4) — every instrument in the final chord is a **one-shot**, deliberately, so nothing loops past this point. That matters technically: MetalKeys and Strings7 are the only two looped samples in the set, and neither is left ringing on any channel here — both get cut by this final trigger, same way a new note on a channel has always silently overridden whatever was sustaining there throughout the piece. Nothing plays after row 45; the one-shots decay entirely on their own, and the piece ends in genuine silence rather than a cut-off loop or a fade.
