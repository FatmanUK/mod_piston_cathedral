## Pattern 06 — "Forge B" (Climax variant)

Kick and bass ostinato stay exactly as they've been since Pattern 01 — same principle as before, foundation locked. Ch1's drone stays silent throughout, continuing from where Forge A left it (no fresh trigger needed).

**Choir of Gears — melodic inversion.** Rather than just picking different notes, I mirrored Forge A's exact phrase (G#3→B-3→D-4→D#4→D-4→B-3→G#3, a "valley then peak" arc) around its own D#4 axis, giving A#4→G-4→E-4→D#4→E-4→G-4→A#4 — a "peak then valley" arc, the true melodic inverse. Same technique Liturgy B used on Liturgy A, applied properly this time with the interval math worked out rather than just picking a different contour.

**Hammerfall — a first for HiHat2.** Instrument 3 hasn't appeared in any pattern yet, so this is its debut: dense 8th-note hihat fills thread between the Snare7+Mechanic2 hits, which themselves shift to syncopated off-beat positions (33/39/43/46) rather than Forge A's more even 32/38/44/45 — a busier, more rushed-feeling variant of the same layered-hit motif.

**Klaxon — descending mirror.** Forge A's Siren climbed F-4→B-4. Here it runs the same six positions in reverse pitch order, B-4 down to F-4, while volume still escalates to full — rising intensity paired with falling pitch, a genuine contrast rather than a repeat.
