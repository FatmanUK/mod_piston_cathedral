## Pattern 04 — "Bridge (Threshold)"

Motif I (Ignition) densifies across the pattern — kick starts at the familiar quarter-note pulse from the Nave/Liturgy patterns, adds one exploratory 8th-note fill in bar 2, moves to full 8th notes in bar 3, and rolls into 16th notes for the last beat and a half of bar 4 — a literal acceleration into the Forge. The bass ostinato (Ch3) stays **identical** to the Nave/Liturgy line throughout — deliberately not varying it, so the sense of "building" comes entirely from the kick and Siren layered on top of a familiar, stable foundation.

Motif IV (Klaxon) becomes insistent: instead of one arc (Pattern 00) or one interjection (Pattern 03), Siren fires 17 times with steadily shrinking gaps and a chromatic ascent (C-3 up to E-4), volume climbing the whole way via `Cxx` — no new effect, reusing what Pattern 00 introduced.

Ch1 (drone) has no new trigger — Strings7 keeps sustaining/looping from earlier patterns, same convention as 02/03.
