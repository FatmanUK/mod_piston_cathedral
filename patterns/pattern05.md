## Pattern 05 — "Forge A" (Climax)

This is the densest pattern yet: kick moves from the quarter-note pulse (Nave/Liturgy) to full 8th notes throughout — the "arrival" the Bridge was building toward. The bass ostinato stays **exactly** as it's been since Pattern 01 (same technique as the Bridge: keep the foundation stable, let everything above it carry the intensity).

**A structural choice worth flagging:** at row 32, Ch1 stops sustaining the Strings7 drone and gets repurposed to trigger Mechanic2 instead — meaning the Bellows drone that's been quietly running since Pattern 00 actually cuts out for the rest of this pattern. That's deliberate: the "sermon" drops the atmospheric backing entirely and lets raw mechanism take over. It won't resume on its own — if you want it back for Requiem or elsewhere, it'll need a fresh trigger there.

**Motif rollout across the four bars:**
- **Bars 1–2 (rows 0–31):** identical repeated cell — dense kick, unchanged bass, and an aggressive Choir of Gears run (G#3→B-3→D-4→D#4→D-4→B-3→G#3) replacing the earlier call-and-response phrasing with something more insistent.
- **Bar 3 (rows 32–47):** Hammerfall enters — Snare7 (Ch4) and Mechanic2 (Ch1) fire **simultaneously** on the same rows, the "layered hit" the motif calls for, with an extra off-grid 16th-note double-hit at row 45 to tighten the syncopation.
- **Bar 4 (rows 48–63):** Siren climbs in a straight chromatic-ish run from F-4 all the way to B-4 — the literal ceiling of its allowed range, deliberately topping out higher than the Bridge's E-4. Volume rides up via `Cxx` to full (`40`) right as it peaks. Mechanic2 keeps a thinning pulse on Ch1 underneath, and a final Snare7 hit on row 63 acts as a pickup into Pattern 06.
